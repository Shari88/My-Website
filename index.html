Our mission

Climate finance has spent decades pricing carbon without understanding the ecosystems that hold it. Gaia builds the measurement, verification, and funding infrastructure that lets capital flow directly to the land and water doing the work — restoring forests, protecting marine habitats, and turning verified carbon outcomes into investable assets.

Forest restoration

We finance reforestation and old-growth protection, verified by satellite monitoring and independent field audits.

Marine ecosystems

Mangroves, seagrass, and kelp store carbon at rates forests can't match — we fund their return at scale.

Carbon finance

We structure verified carbon outcomes into transparent instruments that investors can actually underwrite.
